## 一、这篇讲什么（3-5 句）

【原文】本文提出一套**针对"高维、稀疏、多变量二值时间序列"的转移量化方法**（Behavior Research Methods 55:2960）。【原文】它用**滑动窗口**构造一系列**转移矩阵**，从中导出两个指标：**stability**（转移矩阵的迹 ÷ 矩阵全部元素之和）与 **spread**（矩阵中非零元素个数 ÷ 全部可能元素个数 = k²）【原文，p.2960 摘要，p.2964 式(2)(3)】。【原文】作者做了 1728 组模拟（k = 2/10/20/30，L = 10/25/50/100，W = L 的 .02/.05/.1/.2，N = 20/50/75/100），给出参数下限建议：**W ≥ 5、k ≥ 4、L ≥ 9**，并发现 stability 与 spread 呈中度负相关但各自携带独立信息【原文，p.2965–2968】。【原文】实证例子是 110 名高社交焦虑个体为期 4 周的 EMA：20 种情绪调节策略的勾选（二值）——结果显示策略选择的 stability 能预测下一时刻的焦虑与消极情绪，spread 只能预测消极情绪【原文，p.2974–2975】。

**【推断｜重要定调】这是一篇纯方法论文。** 它的"情绪"内容只是**应用示范**（情绪调节策略的切换计数），**没有任何关于情绪结构、情绪维度的理论结论**。论文自述："these analyses are primarily exploratory"（p.2971）。因此对我们的 PAD / A2 建模，**它唯一可被引用的是方法本身**，不是它的情绪学结论。

## 二、⭐ 核心方法与数学形式（公式逐字 + 中译）

### 2.1 三个公式的逐字形式

**【原文，式(1)，p.2962】** 转移矩阵的定义域：

$$\mathbf{X}_{ij} : i \in \{1, 2, \dots, N\}, \quad j \in \{1, 2, \dots, J_i\}$$

> 中译：$\mathbf{X}_{ij}$ 是"第 i 个人、第 j 个时间窗"的转移矩阵。随后正文说明【原文，p.2962】："Where $\mathbf{X}_{ij}$ is a k x k transition matrix for person i within time window j; k is the number of binary variables to be included in the analysis; N is the number of participants"。
> 中译：$\mathbf{X}_{ij}$ 是 k×k 的转移矩阵；k 是纳入分析的**二值变量个数**；N 是**被试人数**；$J_i$ 是该被试在把观测切分成一系列小窗后能构造出的转移矩阵个数。

**【原文，式(3)，p.2963】** 窗口数与窗长的关系（假定窗间滑动滞后 = 1）：
（⚠️ 原文编号有误：p.2963 的这条标为 (3)，p.2964 的 stability 公式又标为 (2)——即 **(2) 号被用了两次**。本分析按出现顺序转述。）

$$J_i = L_i - W + 1$$

> 中译：$L_i$ 是第 i 个人整条时间序列的观测总数；$W$ 是"贡献给单个转移矩阵的连续观测数"（**窗口大小**，hyperparameter，必须为 ≥ 2 的正整数且不超过观测总数）。【原文】"The value of $J_i$ is determined by W relative to the length of the individual's overall time series and the lag that is set between initial observations of the subseries that construct two successive transition matrices (i.e., the windowing lag)."——即 $J_i$ 由 W、L 与**窗口滑动滞后**（windowing lag）共同决定，上式是滞后为 1 的特例。

**【原文，式(2)，p.2964】** 稳定性（⚠️ 注意：**原文式号有误，把这条与 p.2963 的 $J_i$ 关系式重复编号**——$J_i$ 那条实际标为 (3)，本条标为 (2)，即 **(2) 号被用了两次**。这是排版错误，非本分析笔误）：

$$St_{ij} = \frac{tr(\mathbf{X}_{ij})}{\sum\sum \mathbf{X}_{ij}}, \qquad k = 1 \Rightarrow \text{"noUse"}$$

【原文，p.2964 紧接说明】"Here, $tr(\mathbf{X}_{ij})$ is the sum of the elements along the diagonal of $\mathbf{X}_{ij}$; $\sum\sum \mathbf{X}_{ij}$ is the sum of all elements of $\mathbf{X}_{ij}$; Stability is calculated for each $\mathbf{X}_{ij}$ and is stored as a vector."
> 中译：$tr(\mathbf{X}_{ij})$ 是矩阵**对角线元素之和**；$\sum\sum \mathbf{X}_{ij}$ 是矩阵**全部元素之和**；对每个 $\mathbf{X}_{ij}$ 各算一个 Stability，存成向量。
> ⚠️ 补充：【推断】若 $\sum\sum \mathbf{X}_{ij} = 0$（该窗内没有任何观测到观测的转移），论文【原文，p.2963 脚注 2】说稳定性会返回 "noUse" 解、spread 为 0。R 源码证实这一点（见第五节）。

**【原文，式(3)，p.2964】** 分散度：

$$Sp_{ij} = \frac{nz(\mathbf{X}_{ij})}{k^2}$$

【原文，p.2964】"$nz(\cdot)$ is a count of the number of non-zero elements in $\cdot$; $k^2$ is number of elements in $\mathbf{X}_{ij}$; Spread is calculated for each $\mathbf{X}_{ij}$ and is stored as a vector."
> 中译：$nz(\cdot)$ 统计矩阵中**非零元素的个数**；$k^2$ 是该矩阵的元素总数（即全部可能的转移类型数）；对每个 $\mathbf{X}_{ij}$ 各算一个 Spread。

### 2.2 转移矩阵 $\mathbf{X}_{ij}$ 的构造规则（逐字）

【原文，p.2963】构造算法原文：

> "We first create $\mathbf{X}_{ij}$ with k x k dimensions and initialize all elements to zero. To build $\mathbf{X}_{i1}$, we iterate through person i's subseries of length W and increment elements of $\mathbf{X}_{i1}$ by 1 for each observed transition between k options for all observations within the first subseries. **If the same binary variable (e.g., variable A) was selected at consecutive time points, we increment the diagonal of $\mathbf{X}_{i1}$ in the element (a,a) of the matrix. If two different binary time series variables were selected at consecutive time points (e.g., variable A then variable B), we increment the off-diagonal of $\mathbf{X}_{i1}$ in element (b,a).**"

> 中译：初始化 k×k 全零矩阵 → 遍历长度为 W 的子序列 → 每观测到一次"相邻时刻之间的转移"就把对应元素 +1。**若同一变量 A 在相邻两时刻都被选中 → 对角线 (a,a) +1；若相邻两时刻先 A 后 B → 非对角 (b,a) +1**（注意行列约定：**列 = 前一刻，行 = 下一刻**）。

【原文，p.2963】滑动方式："To build $\mathbf{X}_{i2}$, we iterate through person i's second subseries of length W, starting with their second overall observation and stopping with time series observation W+1. We continue building transition matrices, sliding the subseries window down the length of person i's overall time series by one each time until observation $L_i$ is captured in $\mathbf{X}_{imax(ji)}$."
> 中译：第二个窗从第 2 个观测开始、到第 W+1 个观测结束；以此类推，每次下移一个观测，直到 $L_i$ 被纳入最后一个矩阵。

**【原文，p.2963】关键规则：允许多状态同时被选中**
> "**Unlike traditional Markov models, our method can account for multiple states being endorsed simultaneously.** Additionally, the windowed approach to the data allows for this method to account for non-stationarity inherent in many time series derived from human behavioral data."

**【原文，p.2962 脚注 1】只统计"观测到观测"的转移**
> "we define transitions as those that occur between *endorsed* variables in two successive observations (i.e., observed-to-observed). **We do not treat unobserved-to-observed, unobserved-to-unobserved, or observed-to-unobserved as transitions** because, in the high-dimensional and sparse data systems for which this method was designed, they would far outnumber the observed-to-observed transitions and potentially make it more challenging to detect these less frequent transitions that are of greater interest."

> 中译：只有当相邻两个时刻都有"被选中的变量"时才记转移；"全空 → 有"、"全空 → 全空"、"有 → 全空"都不计为转移。

### 2.4 两个指标在定义上的性质

- 【原文，p.2963】"Stability is a proportion bounded between 0 and 1." /【原文，p.2964】"Spread is a proportion bounded between 0 and 1."
- 【原文，p.2967】"the number of possible spread values for a given transition matrix is $k^2+1$. For example, the only possible spread values when k = 2 are 0, .25, .50, .75, 1."→ **spread 是离散格点值，格数 = k²+1**。
- 【原文，p.2967】"Whereas variance in spread is constrained by k, variance in stability is constrained by the number of observed transitions irrespective of k."
- 【原文，p.2967】随机过程下："as stability approaches 1, spread necessarily converges to $\frac{1}{k^2}$. However, as spread approaches 1, stability converges to $\frac{1}{k}$."
- 【原文，p.2967】**stability 与 spread 只是样本统计量**："these metrics should be treated as sample statistics rather than parameter estimates."

## 三、能回答什么问题（尤其：能否区分一阶/高阶依赖）

### 3.1 它能回答的问题

【原文】作者对两指标的定位（p.2962）：stability 适用于"the extent to which a system transitions from endorsing one binary variable to endorsing a different binary variable is of theoretical interest"；spread 适用于"the diversity of the transition states that are observed within a multivariate binary time series affording many possible transition states is of theoretical interest"。实证解释（p.2972）："A stability value of 1 indicates rigid repetition of the same strategy over time and a value of 0 indicates no repetition of the same strategy across consecutive surveys. Spread values closer to 1 indicate more unique transitions between strategies and values closer to 0 indicate fewer unique transitions between strategies."

【推断】归纳下来它能回答的是这四类"状态序列的形态"问题：

| 能回答 | 指标 | 直觉 |
|---|---|---|
| 系统是**黏住**还是**频繁换手**？ | stability | 对角线占比 = 自我延续比例 |
| 系统**经历过多少种不同的转移类型**（曲目广度）？ | spread | 非零格子数 / k² |
| 同一系统在**时间内部**是否稳定？（非平稳） | 滑动窗口得到 St/Sp 的**时间序列** | 窗口内的变化被保留 |
| St/Sp 能否**预测外部结果**？ | 把 St/Sp 当自变量放进交叉滞后面板模型 | 论文实证示范 |

### 3.2 ⭐⭐ 它能不能区分一阶 vs 高阶依赖？——**不能。这是本文对我们 A2 问题最重要的一条否定结论。**

【推断｜以下四条理由，前两条是原文直接证据，后两条是公式层面的结构推断】

1. **【原文，p.2967】明确自陈只是描述统计量，不是参数估计**："these metrics are based off the observed transition matrix, which implies that their statistical consistency is entirely dependent on the consistency of the observed data… **As such, these metrics should be treated as sample statistics rather than parameter estimates.**" → 全套方法**没有任何似然函数、没有参数、没有标准误、没有模型比较框架**，因此**不可能做嵌套模型检验**。
2. **【原文，p.2967】明确自陈对矩阵内容不敏感**："stability and spread are agnostic to the specific variables included in each row and column of a given transition matrix… they do not differentiate between a person who occupies one off-diagonal element from a person who occupies a different off-diagonal element. **As such, multiple transition patterns could occur that result in the same stability or spread value.**" → 具体转移模式被抹平，更谈不上一阶 vs 高阶的辨识。
3. **【推断】构造本身是"二阶联合计数、一阶条件读数"**：$\mathbf{X}_{ij}$ 的每一个格子来自**且仅来自相邻两个时刻**。它从不把 $t-2$ 的信息带进来，所以它在结构上**无法表达** $P(e_t \mid e_{t-1}, e_{t-2}) \neq P(e_t \mid e_{t-1})$ 这类高阶依赖。窗口 W 只是**把更多的相邻对累加平均**，不是把历史的阶数加长。
4. **【推断】对一阶依赖的捕捉也是"弱而粗糙"的**：stability 高 ⇔ 相邻时刻倾向同一状态 ⇔ 正自相关强，这与 A2 的 $\rho$ 在**方向上是同构的**，但（a）它不给出 $\rho$ 的数值估计；（b）它把连续强度二值化，丢掉幅度信息；（c）它无法与一阶模型做统计比较。所以它最多能作为 A2 的**定性佐证**（"ρ 应该大，因为 stability 高"），**不能作为"一阶够不够"的判据**。

> **结论（对我们的 A2）**：想用这篇的方法回答"A2 的一阶线性形式够不够"，**此路不通**。见第九节 9.5 我给的替代路线。

## 四、数据要求

### 4.1 参数下限（【原文，Table 3，p.2967–2968】，逐项抄录）

| 参数 | 定义（原文） | 推荐下限 | 对平均 stability 的影响 | 对平均 spread 的影响 | 原文明示的注意事项 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **W** | Number of observations that contribute to a given transition matrix | **5** | Little influence | Positive influence | "Avoid setting W to be significantly higher than k, as this would restrict variance in spread by increasing the likelihood to observe each state at least once within the transition matrix" |
| **k** | Number of time series variables that contribute to a given transition matrix | **4** | Negative influence (assuming a random generating process) | Constrains the number of possible spread values for a given transition matrix ($k^2+1$) | "To avoid overly sparse matrices, set larger W values if larger k or allow >1 time series to be endorsed simultaneously" |
| **L** | Number of total observations within a timeseries | **9**（\* = 假定窗口滞后为 1，且关心个体内变化） | Little influence | Little influence | "The total number of transition matrices for individual i is given by L – W + 1. Collect sufficiently long time series relative to chosen W to observe within-person change across transition matrices" |

### 4.2 其他硬性要求与边界

- 【原文，p.2963】"W must be a positive integer ≥ 2 and cannot exceed the number of observations."
- 【原文，p.2965】模拟覆盖范围：**N ∈ {20, 50, 75, 100}，k ∈ {2, 10, 20, 30}，L ∈ {10, 25, 50, 100}，W = {.02,.05,.1,.2}×L**（不足 2 时强制为 2），**窗口滑动滞后一律设为 1**；每格 1000 次；含补充材料共 1728 组，约 3000 CPU 小时。
- 【原文，p.2969】"We show that this method works when analyzing **as many as 30 time series**, the highest value for k included in this initial simulation study."→ **k > 30 未经检验**。
- 【原文，p.2965】"The standard errors of the spread estimates do not appear to monotonically decrease as more observations are included **until W = 5**, which suggests that spread values calculated with fewer than five observations may not be trustworthy."
- 【原文，p.2969】**采样率与等间隔假设**："this method does not account for the amount of real time that passes between successive observations… this method may be best suited to repeated-measures data that are collected with equal time intervals. However, given that we use overlapping sliding windows and time delay embedding has been shown to be robust to sampling interval misspecification (Boker et al., 2018), it is likely that equal interval measurement is not a necessary condition."
- 【原文，p.2968】**跨样本不可比**："raw stability and spread values should not be compared across samples that use different W values and/or different numbers of binary time series."

### 4.3 数据形态要求（【推断】归纳 + 【原文】依据）

- **二值**：每个变量每个时刻 ∈ {0,1}（"each endorsed strategy was coded as a 1 (vs. 0)"，p.2971）。
- **同一时刻可多个变量为 1**：【原文，p.2963】"our method can account for multiple states being endorsed simultaneously"；实证中"Participants were not limited in the number of strategies they could select at each survey"（p.2971）。
- **允许整个时刻全 0**，但**全 0 相关的转移不计入**（脚注 1）。【原文，脚注 2，p.2963】此时研究者有两个选择：**(a)** 加一条"以上皆无"序列使 k = n+1；**(b)** 保持 k = n，那么"if none of the measured states were observed at successive timepoints within a given transition matrix, the stability calculation would return a 'noUse' solution and spread would be 0."
- **缺失值处理**：【推断】论文正文未讨论缺失值插补。实际策略是**不插补**——因为只统计 observed-to-observed 转移，任何"空时刻"两侧的跨空转移自然被排除，等于按"删除该转移"处理。R 函数源码里用 `sum(x[...]) == 0` 判 `"noUse"`（见第五节），与此一致。
- **稀疏识别性**：【原文，p.2961–2962】方法的动机正是"existing binary time series methods are not designed for"高维稀疏数据——Tian & Anderson (2000) 的多元马尔可夫方法"are limited to a small number of variables (i.e., four) because they return nonidentifiable parameters when data are sparse."

**⭐ 源码级发现（重要，论文里没有）：**

1. **包实际计算了第三个指标 `symmetry`（对称性），论文正文完全未提及**。源码（`R/transStats.R`）：
   ```r
   res[k,3] <- ifelse(sum(x[i,,,j])==0, "noUse", sum(diag(x[i,,,j])) / sum(x[i,,,j]))
   res[k,4] <- Matrix::nnzero(x[i,,,j]) / (dim(x)[2]*dim(x)[3])
   Asym  <- (x[i,,,j] + t(x[i,,,j])) * .5
   Aasym <- (x[i,,,j] - t(x[i,,,j])) * .5
   res[k,5] <- ifelse(sum(x[i,,,j])==0, "noUse",
                (norm(Asym, type="2") - norm(Aasym, type="2")) /
                (norm(Asym, type="2") + norm(Aasym, type="2")))
   ```
   → stability 用 `sum(diag)/sum`、spread 用 `nnzero/k²`，**与式(2)(3)完全一致**；`symmetry` 用对称部分与反对称部分的 Frobenius 范数之比，即 A↔B 与 B↔A 的**方向不对称程度**。【推断】这恰好部分回应了论文自己承认的"对行列具体内容不敏感"的缺陷——**如果我们只需要一个可以立刻用的方向性指标，`symmetry` 是白送的。**
2. **`buildTransArray` 只有 `x` 与 `W` 两个参数，没有 `lag`** → 论文提到的"windowing lag"在实现中**被硬编码为 1**。【推断】若我们要用非 1 的滞后，必须改源码。
3. `sum(x[...])==0` 时 stability/spread 返回字符串 `"noUse"` → 【推断】下游建模前必须先过滤，否则会污染数值列。
4. 【推断】`x` 要求"被试 × 时间"的宽格式输入（因为输出第 1 维是"participant"）。

## 六、与其他方法的对比

【原文，p.2961–2962】作者对既有方法的批评与本文定位：

- **Markov / 多元马尔可夫**："Markov models are perhaps the most common class of models used to analyze binary time series outcomes… Tian and Anderson (2000) generalized these procedures to study joint transition probabilities between more than one variable of interest at a time; **however, these procedures are limited to a small number of variables (i.e., four) because they return nonidentifiable parameters when data are sparse.**" + "modeling every possible state combination as its own discrete state quickly becomes computationally intractable. To illustrate, consider that you are interested in 20 binary time series. If each possible state combination is modeled as its own unique state, then 2²⁰ combinations are possible, and the solution quickly becomes intractable."
- **RQA / 交叉递归 / 多维 RQA**："Recurrence quantification analysis (RQA; Webber & Zbilut, 1994) has been used to understand switching patterns in univariate time series… However, the number of time series that can be investigated jointly with multidimensional RQA becomes computationally intractable as the number of time series under study increases."
- **本文的定位**："We propose the current method as one tool for researchers who are interested in studying transitions within large, complex, high-dimensional systems that are too sparse to be effectively analyzed using existing RQA or Markov chain methods."
- **与 RQA 的实证对比（【原文，Table 4，p.2970】）**：1000 组模拟（N=100, k=10, L=100, W=20, lag=1），剔除 stability > 0.4 的 14 个离群点后：

| 变量 | M | SD | 与 Stability 的相关 | 与 Spread 的相关 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Recurrence Rate | 49.49 | 7.56 | −0.08 [−0.09, −0.08] | **−0.49** [−0.49, −0.49] |
| Determinism | 80.72 | 6.54 | −0.11 [−0.12, −0.11] | −0.48 [−0.48, −0.48] |
| Entropy | 1.67 | 0.30 | −0.07 [−0.08, −0.07] | −0.48 [−0.48, −0.48] |
| Stability | 0.11 | 0.01 | — | −0.34 [−0.35, −0.34] |
| Spread | 0.87 | 0.20 | −0.34 [−0.35, −0.34] | — |

（所有相关 p < .001；RQA 指标用 R 的 `crqa` 包，逐对双变量计算后取均值；相关用 `psych` 包计算。）
→ 【原文，p.2970】"The simulation results suggest that stability and spread provide different information about the transition behavior of time series data than does RQA."

**⭐ 对比总表**【推断：综合原文事实 + 方法学常识，非论文原表】：

| 维度 | Stability/Spread（本文） | HMM | 多元 Markov（Tian & Anderson） | VAR / AR | 动态因子模型（DFM） |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 数据 | 多变量**二值**、高维稀疏 | 单/少变量离散状态 | 二值（**≤4 变量**，否则不可识别） | **连续** | 连续、多指标 |
| 参数/可识别性 | **无参数**（描述统计） | 转移矩阵 + 发射分布 | 联合转移概率 | 系数矩阵 | 载荷 + 因子动态 |
| 高维可扩展性 | **k ≥ 30 已验证**，k² 张量但不求逆 | 状态数指数爆炸 | 差（2^k 组合） | 维度高时需正则化 | 需指定因子数 |
| 同时多标签 | **支持**（1 时刻多变量 =1） | 不支持（每时刻一个状态） | 有限支持 | N/A（连续） | N/A |
| 非平稳 | **滑动窗口显式处理** | 需时变 HMM | 一般不处理 | 需时变系数 | 需时变载荷 |
| 统计推断 | **无**（无 SE、无似然） | 有（EM + LRT/BIC） | 有 | 有 | 有 |
| 保留具体转移模式 | **否**（行列无关，作者明说） | 是 | 是 | 是 | 部分 |
| 时间间隔 | **不考虑**（作者列为局限） | 可建连续时间版 | 不考虑 | 可建连续时间版 | 可 |

【推断】**一句话对比**：它不是 VAR/HMM 的替代品，而是"当变量数大到让 Markov/VAR 不可识别时，**还能给你一个可算的、有解释力的形状摘要**"的降维式描述工具。它牺牲了参数可解释性与推断能力，换取高维稀疏可算性。

## 七、⚠️ 作者自陈的局限

以下**全部为原文自陈**（页码标注）：

1. **⚠️ 只是汇总统计量，不是参数估计（p.2967）**："this method… their statistical consistency is entirely dependent on the consistency of the observed data (i.e., the transition matrices and related metrics will accurately depict the transition behavior of the system if and only if the time series data that are fed into the transition matrices accurately capture the transitions within the system). **As such, these metrics should be treated as sample statistics rather than parameter estimates.**"
2. **⚠️ 对行列内容不敏感 → 不同模式可得同一分数（p.2969）**："stability and spread are agnostic to the specific variables included in each row and column of a given transition matrix. Thus… **they do not differentiate between a person who occupies one off-diagonal element from a person who occupies a different off-diagonal element.** As such, multiple transition patterns could occur that result in the same stability or spread value."
3. **⚠️ 使用边界：只有你不在乎"是哪个状态"时才适用（p.2969）**："These metrics are designed to be used when interest is at the level of (in)stability or spread within a system **without strong interest in differentiating between a given level of stability due to one state's frequent endorsement over the same level of stability due to a different state's frequent endorsement.** Future work should seek to identify ways to compare specific patterns of elements observed within transition matrices if which binary options are endorsed over time is of theoretical interest."
4. **⚠️ spread 不反映权重（p.2969）**："our operationalization of spread **does not capture the degree or weight of certain transitions over others.** Rather, its values are affected by whether each possible transition occurred, not by the extent to which each transition type occurred relative to the others. Future method development work may seek to extend the current spread metric to a continuous spread metric." 并建议："Eigenvalues-based methods, matrix rotations, or decompositions, among others, may offer useful additional approaches."
5. **⚠️ 只算 observed-to-observed（p.2969）**："our operationalizations of stability and spread only consider observed-to-observed transitions, irrespective of other potentially interesting transitions afforded by the multivariate binary time series (i.e., observed-to-unobserved, unobserved-to-observed, unobserved-to-observed)." 建议可用分块矩阵（block matrices）补齐四类转移。
6. **⚠️ 不考虑真实流逝时间（p.2969）**："this method does not account for the amount of real time that passes between successive observations… researchers should take care to sample at a rate that best captures the underlying process of interest because sampling frequency may influence the validity of the stability and spread values that are derived. Because this method does not account for elapsed time… this method may be best suited to repeated-measures data that are collected with equal time intervals."（作者随后用 Boker et al. 2018 的时延嵌入稳健性做了一点缓和。）
7. **⚠️ 参数不可跨样本比较（p.2968）**："raw stability and spread values should not be compared across samples that use different W values and/or different number of binary time series. For this reason, researchers should always report the parameter values they select."
8. **⚠️ 建议需配合其他方法（p.2969）**："Additional measures may also be used alongside stability and spread to enhance understanding of the system from different perspectives (e.g., **recurrence rate on dimension-reduced data along with stability**: Wallot, 2019; **Shannon entropy along with spread**: Rajaram et al., 2017)."
9. **⚠️ k 上限未经检验（p.2969）**：最高只验证到 k = 30。
10. **⚠️ 实证例子专属局限（p.2975–2976）**：
    - EMA 采样**时间间隔在被试内与被试间都不等**："the amount of time between observations varied within and between people. **There are open questions as to whether inconsistent sampling rates introduce bias into the outcomes of this method.**"
    - **依从性不完美**导致"W=6 的窗"并不真正对应"同一天"："imperfect compliance resulted in transition matrices that conceptually did not always reflect within-day stability and spread in ER choices."
    - **模型约束**："we constrained all auto-regressive and cross-regressive paths to be equivalent over time. Although this reduced the estimated parameters in the model by over 700…"（牺牲了时变关系）
    - **探索性**："This work is exploratory and should be replicated to prevent overinterpretation."

## 九、⭐ 迁移到我们场景的评估（能不能用、怎么改）

### 9.0 我们的数据（复述任务事实）

单一年度角色（陪伴型 AI）的情绪状态序列：连续 **PAD 三维球内点**（A1，强度 r = ‖E − μ‖）+ **离散八型标签** + **二值强度 strong/mild**（A4，599 词中文情绪词库）；时间单位 = **对话轮次**；待验证/改进的动力学 = **A2：$E_t = \mu + \rho(E_{t-1} - \mu) + \Delta E_t$**。

### 9.1 结论一：**若严格照搬论文设计 → 不可行**，理由是样本量/设计层面的，且很硬

【推断】论文整套效度论证**建立在"多人 × 短序列"的横断面比较上**：模拟的 N ∈ {20,…,100}，实证 n = 110，最终统计推断来自**个体间**的交叉滞后面板模型。**stability 与 spread 本身没有解析标准误**（作者明说它们只是 sample statistics，p.2967），所以它们的"显著性"完全依赖跨个体的变异。

**我们只有"一个角色 + 一条序列"**，因此：
- 只能得到**一条** St/Sp 时间序列，**没有个体间变异** → 无法做论文式的推断；
- 目标量（是否稳定、是否丰富）会**退化为对一条曲线的目视描述**；
- 更致命的：**单序列下 St/Sp 的"变化"无法与采样噪声分离**，因为这些指标是窗口内计数的比值（尤其我们若 k 小、W 小，格点离散度极高）。

> **诚实结论**：**对"单角色单序列"，这套方法不能提供统计推断，只能提供描述性摘要。照搬会得到一个漂亮的图和一个站不住的结论。**

### 9.2 结论二：**改造后可行**——我们的数据其实天然有一个"跨个体"维度被忽略了

【推断｜我们的层级结构】真正的时间序列不是"角色一生一条"，而是：
```
角色（1 个）
 └── 会话 session（多次，s = 1..S）      ← 天然的"person i"
      └── 对话轮次 turn（每会话 L_s 轮）  ← 天然的"time point"
```
**论文的 "person i" 对应我们的 "session s"**，于是：
- **N = 会话数 S**（不是 1）→ 恢复了个体间变异，可以跑论文式面板模型（跨会话层面）；
- **L = 每会话轮次数**，要求 L ≥ 9（W = 5 时）→ 短会话（< 9 轮）直接丢弃或与相邻会话合并；
- 还可以反向做**窗口级重复测量**：把每个滑动窗口当作一个观测单位，St/Sp 的时间序列即"角色情绪曲线的形状轨迹"，用来做变点检测（角色的情绪切换模式何时变了）。

【推断】如果连会话数都不够（例如只有一两次长会话），**最后一招**是把"每个会话"进一步切成"每个固定长度的连续对话段"作为 pseudo-subject——但这已在扭曲方法前提（段与段之间不独立），必须在论文里明确声明为权宜之计。

### 9.3 具体二值化方案：我们有**两条**互不干扰的可用通道，不需要硬凑

**通道 ⭐A（主力）：八型标签 → k = 8 个二值变量**
- 变量 j = "第 t 轮是否属于第 j 种情绪型"（one-hot 或 one-hot+共现 → 严格按论文，允许一轮多型同时为 1）。
- k = 8 ≥ 4 ✓（论文下限）；k = 8 时可能的 spread 取值数 = k²+1 = **65 档**，足够连续。
- 语义：
  - **stability = 情绪型的"黏性"**（角色是否在同一情绪型里连续待着 / 情绪惯性、僵化）；
  - **spread = 情绪型的"曲目广度"**（角色在窗口内用过多少种不同的情绪型转移）。
- 【推断】这两者恰好对应陪伴型角色的两个可直接观测的质量维度：**"情绪是否过于粘滞（输出单调）" vs "情绪是否爆发式乱跳（人设不稳）"**——而且这两个诊断本身对产品就有价值，独立于 A2 是否成立。

**通道 ⭐B：二值强度 strong/mild → 单变量转移矩阵（但要改造，见下）**
- ⚠️ **不能直接用 k=1**：论文要求 k ≥ 4，k=1 时 spread 恒为 1（或 0），完全无变异。
- 【推断｜推荐改造】把"强度"改造成**多状态而非二值**：例如把状态集定义为 **{mild, strong} × 少量情绪型**（取 top-3 情绪型 × 2 强度 = 6 个状态，k = 6 ≥ 4），或更直接：**把 599 词库的强度标签当作一条独立的、与情绪型并行的转移通道**，与通道 A 拼成 k = 8 + 2 = 10 的联合矩阵。此时矩阵同时包含"换型"与"换强度"两类转移，stability 与 spread 是同质的混合量——【推断】**若想分开解读，建议分别算两个矩阵，而不是拼在一起**（因为论文明确说指标对矩阵内容不敏感，混合后无法归因）。
- 【推断】若不想要 8 型，也可以抽象到 PAD 的**离散八型**（喜悦/信任/恐惧/惊讶/悲伤/厌恶/愤怒/期待）作 k=8，那就是通道 A 本身。

**通道 C（可选）：事件二值序列**
【推断】"是否触发某事件（如角色主动发起新话题、用户负反馈）"这类 per-turn 0/1 序列，**是一个真正的单变量二值序列**，恰好落在论文方法"难处理"的那一头（k=1）。**不要单独用它**，而是把它**作为第 k+1 个变量并入通道 A 的矩阵**（k = 9），这样它贡献的是"情绪型 ↔ 事件"的转移格子——这才是它有意义的位置。

### 9.4 参数设定建议（针对我们）

| 参数 | 建议值 | 依据（原文/推断） |
|---|---|---|
| **W（窗长）** | **5**（起步）；若单会话轮次多，可试 6/8 做敏感性分析 | 【原文】"we recommend that researchers set W to be greater than or equal to 5"（p.2968）；且 W < 5 时 spread 的 SE 不随观测增加而下降（p.2965） |
| **k（变量数）** | **8**（八型）或 **9**（八型 + 1 个事件变量） | 【原文】k ≥ 4；k = 8 → 65 档 spread |
| **L（每会话轮次）** | **≥ 9**，不足则弃用该会话 | 【原文】"we recommend a minimum of L = 9 observations to allow for five different stability and spread values per person"（p.2969） |
| **windowing lag** | **1** | 【原文】所有模拟与实证都用 1；且 R 包只实现了 1（第 5.2 节） |
| **每次分析的样本单位** | **每一个会话 = 一个 "person"** | 【推断】见 9.2 |
| **窗口是否可跨会话** | **禁止** | 【推断】防止把长间隔当成一次转移（原文警告：方法不考虑流逝时间，p.2969） |
| **窗口是否可跨长时间沉默** | **禁止**（在会话内也按"连续互动段"切分） | 【推断】同源理由：我们的轮次间隔极不均匀（秒级 vs 跨天），而论文假设大致等间隔 |

【推断】与论文的对照：论文的 W = 6 是因为他们"每天采样 6 次"；**我们的 W = 5 应对应"最近 5 轮对话"**——这是一个有产品直觉的量（约等于一到两个来回的对话片段），比"固定 5 分钟"更贴合对话的自然节奏。

### 9.5 ⭐ 关于 A2："一阶线性够不够"——这篇帮不上，替代路线在此

**必须先说清楚**：【推断】A2 的 $\rho$ 是**连续状态空间下的一阶自回归系数**，而本文的 transition matrix 是**离散状态空间下的相邻对计数比值**。两者是**不同层面**的东西。St/Sp **不能**用来检验 A2 的一阶形式是否充分——第三节 3.2 已给出四条理由（无似然、无参数、对模式不敏感、构造上只含相邻两个时刻）。

**但可以用它做一件有用的事（【推断】）**：把 St/Sp 当作 **A2 之外的增量预测变量**，复刻论文实证的建模格式：

```
下一轮情绪（PAD 点 / r / 八型）× 上一轮情绪 + St/Sp（来自最近 5 轮的窗口）
→ 看 St/Sp 的系数是否显著
```
若显著 → 说明"**情绪的转移结构形态**"携带了 A2 的自回归项没捕捉到的信息 → **这是 A2 需要扩展的真实证据**（不是在"一阶 vs 二阶"的意义上，而是在"线性点预测不足"的意义上）。这正是论文实证做的事（p.2972 的 Analytic Approach），可以原样搬。

**若确实要回答"一阶 vs 高阶"**，【推断】只能另起炉灶，可选：
1. **在离散标签上直接做嵌套似然比检验**：比较 $P(e_t \mid e_{t-1})$ 与 $P(e_t \mid e_{t-1}, e_{t-2})$（即把本文的 k×k 计数矩阵**升级为 k²×k 的二阶转移张量**）。这在本文框架外，且会让矩阵稀疏到 k²·k = 512（k=8）格，**单一角色数据几乎必然不可识别**——这正是论文一开始在批评 Tian & Anderson 的那个陷阱。
2. **在连续 PAD 上做 VAR 阶数选择**：AR(1) vs AR(2) 用 BIC/AIC 或交叉验证比较。这是标准做法，与本文无关。
3. **交叉验证式检验**：用 A2（一阶）与 A2+二阶项分别做下一轮预测，比较样本外误差。【推断】对单角色序列，这是最实际、最不需要识别性假设的路径。

> **【推断】一句话**：**"A2 是否够" 应该用 A2 自己的语言（预测误差 / 信息准则）去问；本文的语言（转移统计量）问的是另一个问题。**

### 9.6 如果要做，具体怎么改（伪代码，约 30 行，无需依赖 R 包）

【推断】既然 R 包 lag 固定为 1、还是未上架的 2020 年代码，建议**直接用 NumPy 自己实现**，顺便把论文的对称性扩展（R 包里的 `symmetry`）也加上：

```python
import numpy as np

def transition_matrix(binary: np.ndarray, W: int):
    """binary: (W, k) 0/1。列=前一刻、行=下一刻，与原文约定一致。
       返回 k×k 计数矩阵；无 observed-to-observed 转移则为 None。"""
    X = np.zeros((binary.shape[1], binary.shape[1]), dtype=int)
    for t in range(W - 1):
        prev, curr = binary[t], binary[t + 1]
        p_idx, c_idx = np.flatnonzero(prev), np.flatnonzero(curr)
        if p_idx.size == 0 or c_idx.size == 0:
            continue                      # 原文脚注 1：含空时刻的转移不计
        for a in p_idx:
            for b in c_idx:
                X[b, a] += 1              # (b, a) = 从 a 到 b
    return X if X.sum() > 0 else None

def stability(X):
    return np.trace(X) / X.sum()                                    # 式(2)

def spread(X):
    return np.count_nonzero(X) / X.size                             # 式(3)

def symmetry(X):                                                    # R 包有、论文未提
    A, Aa = (X + X.T) * .5, (X - X.T) * .5
    na, nb = np.linalg.norm(A), np.linalg.norm(Aa)
    return (na - nb) / (na + nb)

def stability_spread_series(binary_full, W=5):
    """binary_full: (L, k)，返回长度 L-W+1 的 St/Sp/Sym 序列"""
    out = []
    for start in range(len(binary_full) - W + 1):
        X = transition_matrix(binary_full[start:start + W], W)
        if X is None:
            out.append((np.nan, 0.0, np.nan))       # 原文：noUse / spread=0
        else:
            out.append((stability(X), spread(X), symmetry(X)))
    return np.array(out)

def to_ts(binary_full, W=5):
    return binary_full.sum(axis=1) > 0          # 论文脚注 2 的 (a)：加"以上皆无"通道
```

【推断】自检锚点：用 2.3 节的 6×4 示例数据跑 `stability_spread_series(..., W=5)`，**必须得到 St = [3/7, 3/6]、Sp = [6/16, 4/16]**。跑不出这个数就是实现错了。

### 9.7 【推断】迁移前必须接受的三个"认知损失"

1. **PAD 连续性被丢掉了**。八型标签 + strong/mild 是**二值映射**，A1 里"球内点到 μ 的距离 r"这个最有信息量的连续量，在本文框架里**无处安放**（最多二值化成 strong/mild，损失巨大）。所以本文方法**只能作为 A1/A2 的旁证，不能作为主分析**。
2. **它不看"哪个情绪型"**。作者明说对行列内容不敏感（p.2969）。因此"角色从愤怒→悲伤"和"从喜悦→期待"对 stability/spread 是**等价的**。若我们真的在乎"是哪个情绪型在切换"，必须补 R 包里的 `symmetry`（方向性）或图论/熵类指标。
3. **它不修正时间间隔**。对话轮次的间隔方差远大于 EMA 抽样的间隔方差。若不严格限制"单会话内连续互动段"，会引入**不可量化的偏误**（作者自己承认"open questions as to whether inconsistent sampling rates introduce bias"，p.2976）。

### 9.8 迁移结论一览

| 问题 | 回答 |
|---|---|
| 严格照搬（单角色单序列、当作一个人的一条序列做推断） | ❌ **不可行**——无个体间变异、无解析标准误，得不到可推断的结论 |
| 改造后（会话当 person、八型当 k=8 变量、单会话内连续段当窗口） | ✅ **可行**，且有产品意义（诊断情绪黏性/广度） |
| 用于验证 A2 的一阶形式 | ❌ **不可行**（第三节 3.2 四条理由） |
| 用于给 A2 做增量检验（St/Sp 作额外预测变量） | ✅ **可行**（复刻论文 p.2972 的方案） |
| 处理二值变量（词库 strong/mild、事件触发）的能力 | ✅ 正是本方法的设计目标；但**必须 k ≥ 4**，k=1 的二值序列要并入其他变量 |

## 十、原文值得逐字保留的句子（带页码）

> 页码 = 期刊页码（已用渲染页与 PyMuPDF 定位核对）。

**（1）两指标的定义【p.2960 摘要】**
> "We define stability as the trace of a transition matrix divided by the sum of all observed elements within that matrix. We define spread as the number of all non-zero cells in a transition matrix divided by the number of all possible cells in that matrix."

**（2）方法的适用定位【p.2961】**
> "While Markov models and RQA have made exciting contributions to the study of complex multivariate time series, they were not designed to analyze high-dimensional multivariate binary data."

**（3）高维不可解的直接理由【p.2961】**
> "consider that you are interested in 20 binary time series. If each possible state combination is modeled as its own unique state, then 2^20 combinations are possible, and the solution quickly becomes intractable."

**（4）stability 的用途【p.2962】**
> "This metric is useful when the extent to which a system transitions from endorsing one binary variable to endorsing a different binary variable is of theoretical interest."

**（5）spread 的用途【p.2962】**
> "This metric is useful when the diversity of the transition states that are observed within a multivariate binary time series affording many possible transition states is of theoretical interest."

**（6）只计 observed-to-observed（脚注 1）【p.2962】**
> "We do not treat unobserved-to-observed, unobserved-to-unobserved, or observed-to-unobserved as transitions because, in the high-dimensional and sparse data systems for which this method was designed, they would far outnumber the observed-to-observed transitions and potentially make it more challenging to detect these less frequent transitions that are of greater interest."

**（7）同时多状态 + 非平稳【p.2963】**
> "Unlike traditional Markov models, our method can account for multiple states being endorsed simultaneously. Additionally, the windowed approach to the data allows for this method to account for non-stationarity inherent in many time series derived from human behavioral data."

**（8）增量计数规则【p.2963】**
> "If the same binary variable (e.g., variable A) was selected at consecutive time points, we increment the diagonal of X_i1 in the element (a,a) of the matrix. If two different binary time series variables were selected at consecutive time points (e.g., variable A then variable B), we increment the off-diagonal of X_i1 in element (b,a)."

**（9）stability 是"稳定转移的比例"【p.2964】**
> "It is defined as the trace of a transition matrix divided by the sum of all elements within that matrix, and thus is the proportion of transitions that are stable."

**（10）⚠️ 它只是样本统计量，不是参数估计【p.2967】**
> "Notably, these metrics are based off the observed transition matrix, which implies that their statistical consistency is entirely dependent on the consistency of the observed data (i.e., the transition matrices and related metrics will accurately depict the transition behavior of the system if and only if the time series data that are fed into the transition matrices accurately capture the transitions within the system). As such, these metrics should be treated as sample statistics rather than parameter estimates."

**（11）随机过程下的收敛关系【p.2967】**
> "assuming a random process, as stability approaches 1, spread necessarily converges to 1/k^2. However, as spread approaches 1, stability converges to 1/k."

**（12）⭐ 对行列内容不敏感的自我承认【p.2969】**
> "stability and spread are agnostic to the specific variables included in each row and column of a given transition matrix. Thus, while spread and stability summarize the diversity and order in which a person selects between a set of binary variables over time, respectively, they do not differentiate between a person who occupies one off-diagonal element from a person who occupies a different off-diagonal element. As such, multiple transition patterns could occur that result in the same stability or spread value."

**（13）⭐ 使用边界（也是对我们最有用的一句警告）【p.2969】**
> "These metrics are designed to be used when interest is at the level of (in)stability or spread within a system without strong interest in differentiating between a given level of stability due to one state's frequent endorsement over the same level of stability due to a different state's frequent endorsement."

**（14）spread 不反映权重【p.2969】**
> "our operationalization of spread does not capture the degree or weight of certain transitions over others. Rather, its values are affected by whether each possible transition occurred, not by the extent to which each transition type occurred relative to the others."

**（15）⚠️ 不考虑流逝时间【p.2969】**
> "Finally, this method does not account for the amount of real time that passes between successive observations. Similarly, the statistical consistency of stability and spread are entirely dependent on the consistency of the sample transition matrix. As such, researchers should take care to sample at a rate that best captures the underlying process of interest because sampling frequency may influence the validity of the stability and spread values that are derived. Because this method does not account for elapsed time, and given sampling frequency may influence derived values, this method may be best suited to repeated-measures data that are collected with equal time intervals."

**（16）与 RQA 的差异【p.2970】**
> "The simulation results suggest that stability and spread provide different information about the transition behavior of time series data than does RQA."

**（17）实证的解释锚点【p.2972】**
> "A stability value of 1 indicates rigid repetition of the same strategy over time and a value of 0 indicates no repetition of the same strategy across consecutive surveys. Spread values closer to 1 indicate more unique transitions between strategies and values closer to 0 indicate fewer unique transitions between strategies."

**（18）⚠️ 已知偏误风险（实证局限）【p.2976】**
> "like many datasets collected through EMA, the amount of time between observations varied within and between people. There are open questions as to whether inconsistent sampling rates introduce bias into the outcomes of this method."

**（19）⚠️ 窗与理论单位的错位【p.2976】**
> "although W was set to 6 because ER was measured six times daily, imperfect compliance resulted in transition matrices that conceptually did not always reflect within-day stability and spread in ER choices."

**（20）三条推荐下限【p.2977 结论】**
> "the simulation results point to recommended guidelines for setting mathematically and theoretically principled minimum parameter values for: i) the number of observations to be included in a given transition matrix (W ≥ 5); ii) the number of timeseries variables to be included in a given transition matrix (k ≥ 4); and iii) the minimum length of the overall timeseries data, assuming a windowing lag of one and interest in time-varying stability and spread questions (L ≥ 9)."
